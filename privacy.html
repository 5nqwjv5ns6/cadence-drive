PRIVACY

Cadence Drive · Unethical Studios · updated November 8, 2026

Cadence Drive runs entirely in your browser. There are no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no third-party trackers on this site.

What stays on your device

Your settings, garage choices and driving totals are saved in your browser's local storage so they are there next time. They are not sent to us.

Anonymous play statistics

To see how many people play and how well the game runs, the game sends a few anonymous events to our own server: when the page opens, when the world has loaded, when you start driving, a short update about once a minute while you drive, and when you leave. These include:

  • a random id made by your browser (not linked to your name, email or account) and a random id for the visit
  • the game version, which vehicle, camera, weather and graphics settings are in use, frame rate, speed and distance driven
  • device type, operating system, browser, screen size, language and graphics card name
  • the site that linked you here, if any, and your country (worked out by our host from your connection; your IP address is not stored)
  • error messages, if the game hits a problem

To reset the random id, clear this site's data in your browser. Statistics are only collected on this official site.

Issue reports and benchmarks

If you press Report issue or run a benchmark in the game, a technical report is sent to us: your note (if you write one), the settings and position in the world, performance figures, graphics card, browser details and screen size. Don't put personal information in the note.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes connection data to deliver the page and protect it from abuse.
